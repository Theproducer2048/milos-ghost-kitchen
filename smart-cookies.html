Smart Cookies Program

Founded by Danielle Tillman. Good grades taste sweet — celebrating hardworking students in Cleveland and the Houston Area!

Program Rules & Details

  • ⭐ Program Start Date: Monday, November 16, 2026.
  • ⭐ Who Qualifies: Any student with an A/B Honor Roll report card or grades improved since their last report card.
  • ⭐ The Reward: ONE free signature cookie per student per drop!
  • ⭐ How to Redeem: Show the report card at pickup (paper copy or on a phone).
  • ⭐ Requirements: The student must be present in person with a parent or guardian.
  • ⭐ Drop Limit: First 25 students each drop, while supplies last.
