Milo's Smart Cookie Program celebrates hardworking students.

How Smart Cookies works

  • Program starts November 16, 2026.
  • Students qualify with A/B Honor Roll or improved grades since their last report card.
  • One free signature cookie per student per drop.
  • Show the report card at pickup, on paper or on a phone.
  • The student must attend pickup with a parent or guardian.
  • Limited to the first 25 students each drop, while supplies last.

No drop date is currently announced. Text us for program and pickup availability.
