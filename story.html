Our Story.

Milo’s Ghost Kitchen is a Texas cottage-food bakery rooted in the Houston Area and serving Cleveland, Texas (77327, Liberty County). Run by Darius Tillman (Sole Proprietor) and Danielle Tillman, everything we do is family-built.

The bakery is named after Milo, one of our two beloved cats (Milo and Maisie). That’s why everything stays cat-themed, playful, and full of warmth.

Danielle runs our menu curation, design collaborations, and our signature Smart Cookies student incentive program. Darius manages bakery systems, pricing, marketing, and kitchen operations. Both of us cook, backed by ANAB Food Handler certification and Texas DSHS Cottage Food Registry License #20213.

We are strictly pickup-only, never publicize our home kitchen address, and welcome our neighbors with open arms: “Come hungry, leave family.”
